# Content provenance

This package is an original operational framework informed by public principles and articles on [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/), a project by Jon Yablonski, and separately referenced accessibility/design guidance. The source materials remain the property of their respective authors and publishers; no original article bodies, screenshots, poster artwork, or book excerpts are redistributed. Source URLs are preserved throughout the structured catalog so reviewers can inspect context.

This pack is provided for reuse of the **newly authored instructions and metadata**. No license grant for third-party source content is implied. Review licensing before redistributing third-party materials or adding extracts from external pages.
