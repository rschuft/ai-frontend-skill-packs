# End-to-end journeys, interruptions and remembered experience

## Map before polishing

Write the user's goal, trigger, start state, critical decision points, waiting, irreversible moments, recovery and completion. Identify emotional peaks (stress, delight, uncertainty) and handoffs between pages/services. User journeys are not merely screenshot tours.

## Root causes and solutions

1. **Abandoned long flows:** Record each demanded step and whether its information is essential. Remove redundant work, use reliable autofill, disclose remaining work, preserve drafts and resume state.
2. **Lost progress:** Persist meaningful work, show accurate step and saved status, retain context after interruptions, and let users abandon the task without pressure. Zeigarnik is a diagnostic lens, not a license for nagging.
3. **High-stakes peaks:** Make consequences, validation and recovery especially clear around publish, pay, delete, send and authorize. Put the user in control.
4. **Bad endings:** Confirm actual durable result, summary and next steps. Positive visual flourish should not obscure verification or follow-up obligations.
5. **Broken flow:** Minimize nonessential modals, unexpected reauthentication, jitter and delays, especially in concentrated expert tasks. Offer challenge aligned with skill where learning/exploration is the goal.
6. **Prioritization:** Use journey data to select high-value repairs, but explicitly escalate low-frequency accessibility, data-loss, security and trust harms.

## Journey evidence and metrics

A useful journey map contains persona/segment, goal, stages, actions, expectations, feelings, pain points, evidence, opportunity, owner and target metric. Evaluate success and recovery rates, end-to-end task time, near-end drop-off, unwanted returns, perceived control and post-task confidence. Avoid retention or time spent as sole outcomes; fast task completion may be the right experience.

## Safeguards

Never fabricate progress, add artificial urgency, move the finish line, use guilt to force re-entry, or optimize memorable delight while tolerating severe failures. If progress cannot be estimated, communicate state honestly.
