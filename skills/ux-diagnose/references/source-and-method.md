## Knowledge and evidence levels

1. **Measured:** user study, accessibility test, instrumented interaction, or reproducible runtime observation with conditions.
2. **Observed:** screenshot or test session confirms a visible symptom, but not necessarily its cause.
3. **Code-derived:** component behavior inferred from source; verify runtime when possible.
4. **Hypothesized:** plausible risk without observed occurrence; label explicitly and recommend a discriminating test.

Psychological laws are lenses for diagnosis, not proof of causal attribution. Heuristic targets (including Doherty's 400ms) are not automatically engineering acceptance thresholds. Research about averages must not become universal user limits (including Miller's 7±2). Accessibility standards and informed user consent override behavioral optimization. Avoid coercive defaults, fake progress, manipulative scarcity and hidden alternatives.
